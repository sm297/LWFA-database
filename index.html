About this database

A compilation of experimental laser-wakefield-accelerator (LWFA) results: laser and plasma parameters mapped to electron-beam and betatron/γ-source properties. It contains 586 records from 266 papers (2004–2026).

Sources. (1) v1 manual: the hand-curated database behind S.P.D. Mangles, arXiv:1705.10588, cleaned and merged. (2) v3 extracted: values extracted with a large language model from the full text of papers found by a Scopus search (titles screened for experimental LWFA electron-beam or radiation-source results). Each extracted value carries a short supporting quote from the paper, shown when you click a point.

Caveats. Extracted values have not yet been individually reviewed. Automated checks flag inconsistent records (red outline): supporting quote not found verbatim, P≠E/τ, stated a₀ vs a₀ from P and spot size, values outside physical ranges. Papers report best shots, averages or ranges differently (see "value type"). About half the papers in the search are not yet included because their full text was not available. Always check the original paper (DOI link) before relying on a value.

Download: LWFA_database_v3.csv. Corrections are welcome.
